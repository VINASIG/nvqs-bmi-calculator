# Military BMI and physique source review

Reviewed **3 October 2026**. Vietnamese law is authoritative; English text is explanatory. The tool reports a reference grade from entered physique indicators, not the overall medical grade or an individual call-up decision.

## Recruitment standards

[Government publication of Circular 68/2025](https://xaydungchinhsach.chinhphu.vn/thong-tu-68-2025-tt-bqp-sua-doi-bo-sung-mot-so-dieu-ve-tuyen-chon-va-goi-cong-dan-nhap-ngu-119250707223401315.htm) and [full transcription](https://xaydungchinhsach.chinhphu.vn/tuyen-chon-va-goi-cong-dan-nhap-ngu-sua-doi-bo-sung-quy-dinh-ve-trach-nhiem-cua-dia-phuong-giao-quan-119250708001023475.htm) amend Circular 148/2018 Article 4(3). Effective **1 July 2025**:

- Article 1(1)(a), amending Article 4(3)(a): recruit health grades 1, 2 or 3 according to Circular 105 Articles 5 and 6.
- Article 1(1)(b), amending Article 4(3)(c): BMI **below 18.0 or above 29.9** falls within cases not called for enlistment into the military. Equality is inside this separate BMI criterion. Myopia over 1.5 diopters and hyperopia at any level are separate exclusions; this calculator does not examine eyesight.

State out-of-range exclusion directly. A generic disclaimer must not imply another examination result overrides it. Official recorded measurements matter; discrepancies require verification or review.

## Physique table and whole-unit measurement scoring

[Government full text of Circular 105/2023](https://xaydungchinhsach.chinhphu.vn/toan-van-thong-tu-quy-dinh-kham-suc-khoe-cho-cac-doi-tuong-thuoc-quan-ly-cua-bo-quoc-phong-119231211181442997.htm), effective **1 January 2024**, supplies Appendix I Section I. Each indicator scores 1–6; Article 6 uses the highest score. The UI reproduces all six rows. Missing male chest is disclosed; female chest is not scored.

**Appendix I Section IV.1.a** rounds height, weight and chest to whole units, fractions from 0.5 upward. Verified examples include 152.5 cm → 153 cm, 158.49 cm → 158 cm, 46.5 kg → 47 kg and 51.49 kg → 51 kg. Show raw and scoring measurements. BMI arithmetic uses entered values exactly; no unverified official BMI rounding practice is claimed.

**Appendix I Section IV.1.b** describes chest measurement and BMI separately. BMI equals weight (kg) divided by squared height (m); chest is not part of that formula. Chest is an independent male-table physique indicator in Section I. For example, 170 cm / 55 kg yields BMI approximately 19.03 whether chest is omitted, 81 cm or 70 cm. A 70 cm chest scores 6 and raises the reference physique grade to 6, while BMI and its direct recruitment criterion remain unchanged. The UI and unit/browser regressions distinguish these outcomes explicitly.

Relevant signed pages 3–8 were rendered and opened. Effective **30 September 2025**, it changes other provisions/forms, not the Section I physique table or Section IV.1.a rounding.

### BMI 18.0 to below 18.5

Exact BMI at least 18.0 and below 18.5 is inside the Circular 68 BMI criterion but scores **4** under Circular 105. It fails the grade 1–3 recruitment standard. Show both conclusions. Example: 170 cm / 52.6 kg gives approximately 18.20, not excluded by the direct BMI clause but excluded by the physique standard.

### Literal sub-tenth gaps

Printed ranges end at 24.9, 26.9, 29.9, 34.9 and 39.9; subsequent ranges start at 25.0, 27.0, 30.0, 35.0 and 40.0. No verified source supplied a BMI rounding rule to fill those gaps. Preserve the table. Mark BMI score unknown and show adjacent possible reference grades unless a definite other indicator fixes the maximum. Require examining-authority clarification. Exact 18.0/29.9 exclusion remains independent: BMI 29.95 is excluded even if its table score is unresolved. This conservative arithmetic interpretation is not a new legal rule.

## Display precision, uncertainty and safe health references

Input decimals use scaled integers and exact rational comparisons. Prominent BMI has one decimal place. Separate comparison text adds enough decimals to preserve every threshold relation. Never classify on the prominent rounded number.

Threshold weights equal BMI × entered height in meters squared. Show distance from current weight. Illustrative uncertainty evaluates extreme corners of **±0.5 cm and ±0.5 kg**, warning if they cross 18.0 or 29.9. This is not a validated device error estimate or legal tolerance. Recommend official remeasurement and correct recording; threshold weights are not weight-change goals.

Adult health references only use BMI **18.5–24.9**, lower weight rounded up and upper down to 0.1 kg. Underweight guidance seeks professional assessment and nutritious regular meals, never further loss. [NHS healthy weight gain](https://www.nhs.uk/live-well/healthy-weight/managing-your-weight/healthy-ways-to-gain-weight/) supports this general guidance. Adult categories are unsuitable below 20 or during pregnancy; military scoring itself is not restricted to that adult-health age range. No growth goals, diagnosis or individual prescription are given.
